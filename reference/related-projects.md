# Related projects

Platforms and collections close to what Atlas Antiqua wants to be.

- **[Cesium](https://cesium.com) (CesiumJS / Cesium ion):** the closest
  technical foundation. A 3D globe with time-dynamic (4D) data, polygons,
  splines, 3D Tiles for photogrammetry, Gaussian splat support, and AR/VR via
  WebXR or Unreal/Unity plugins.
- **[Google Earth](https://earth.google.com):** zoom from orbit to ground with
  3D terrain and some heritage tours, but no real archaeological database or
  time axis.
- **[ARIADNEplus](https://ariadne-infrastructure.eu):** aggregates millions of
  archaeological records across Europe and beyond, searchable on a map with
  time filters.
- **[Arches](https://www.archesproject.org):** open-source heritage inventory
  platform used by many national agencies, with polygons and dating, but 2D.
- **[CyArk](https://www.cyark.org), [Zamani Project](https://zamaniproject.org),
  [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com):** high-quality scans and photogrammetry
  of sites, viewable in AR/VR, but not tied into one global map.
- **[PeriodO](https://perio.do) and [Pleiades](https://pleiades.stoa.org):**
  shared standards for time periods and places, which a 4D map would need.
